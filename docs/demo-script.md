# Demo script — the KhanijDrishti narrative

A ~4-minute judge walkthrough. Primary story: **Lithium**; secondary: **Rare Earth Elements**.
Everything shown is deterministic demo data, labelled as such.

1. **Landing** (`/`) — "See the Mineral. Map the Technology. Find the Gap." Click **Explore Intelligence**.
2. **Command Center** (`/command`) — one-glance picture: KPIs (30 critical minerals, patent families,
   R&D, technology nodes, organisations, evidence records), the technology activity timeline, the
   mineral × value-chain heatmap, emerging technologies, and potential gap signals. Note the demo banner.
3. **Open Lithium** (`/minerals/lithium`) — the mineral intelligence profile: hero metrics, the
   interactive value chain, technology landscape, patent & R&D activity, leading organisations.
4. **Click Direct Lithium Extraction** (`/technologies/direct-lithium-extraction`) — technology growth,
   maturity, momentum, related patents/R&D, and the emerging component breakdown.
5. **Emerging Radar** (`/emerging`) — where DLE, magnet recycling and battery recycling sit on the
   evidence-based lifecycle (not a TRL claim).
6. **Gap Intelligence** (`/gaps`) → open the **Direct Lithium Extraction** signal (`/gaps/gap-dle-li`).
   High strategic need + high global momentum + lower Indian patent evidence → *Potential Technology
   Opportunity*. Show confidence + evidence sufficiency.
7. **Explain Signal / Why this?** — the weighted breakdown (strategic relevance 38%, momentum 27%,
   domestic capability 19%, evidence sufficiency 16%), supporting evidence counts, and data limitations.
8. **Trace to Evidence** — click an evidence citation → the Evidence & Provenance drawer → open a patent
   (`/patents/kd-p-0001`): technology fingerprint, patent activity signal, classification "why".
9. **Knowledge Graph** (`/graph`) — Lithium → DLE → patent → organisation → publication; toggle node
   types; use *Explore path*.
10. **Collaboration** (`/collaboration`) — a *potential complementary capability* signal (e.g. an
    extraction-strong org + a separation-strong org).
11. **Policy Brief** (`/policy-briefs`) — generate the Lithium intelligence brief; export JSON/PDF.
12. **Ask KhanijDrishti** (`/ask`) — "Which technologies have growing research activity but limited
    Indian patent evidence?" → grounded answer with evidence, confidence, sources, limitations.

**Close:** every insight traced to evidence; signals never overclaim; runs with no live APIs; live
adapters can be added later. Switch mineral to **Rare Earth Elements** to show the same pipeline end-to-end.
